## 3.3 Portfolios, programmes, projects and other related work

> A **portfolio** comprises part or all of an organisation's investment
> required to achieve its objectives.

> A **programme** is a unique, temporary, flexible organisation created to
> co-ordinate, direct and oversee the implementation of a set of projects and
> other related work to deliver outcomes and benefits related to a set of
> strategic objectives.

> A **project** is a unique, temporary management environment, undertaken in
> stages, created for the purpose of delivering one or more business products
> or outcomes.

> A **work package** is a set of information relevant to the creation of one or
> more deliverables or outputs.

The standard sets these out as a nesting: portfolios contain programmes and
projects, which in turn contain work packages, and it notes that work
components may cross organisational and departmental boundaries.

## 7.3 Benefits management

> Benefits management ensures that the measurable value and other positive
> impacts resulting from the work are identified, planned and managed so that
> they can be realised.
